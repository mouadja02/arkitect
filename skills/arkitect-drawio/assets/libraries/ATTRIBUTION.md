# Icon attribution

Generated by `scripts/write-pack-docs.mjs` from `sources.json`.

Every icon in these libraries belongs to whoever made it. Arkitect redistributes
them under the terms below and claims no ownership of any of them. **A trademark is
not licensed by the licence on its artwork**: Simple Icons is CC0, but the marks it
draws are still the property of the companies they identify, and are included here
for nominative use - identifying a product in an architecture diagram.

Two kinds of terms appear below, and the difference matters:

- **Licence** - a licence grant (CC0, MIT, ISC). Redistribution is permitted on the
  licence terms.
- **Permission** - not a licence. Microsoft, Google and AWS permit their icons to be
  used in architecture diagrams and reserve every other right, including the right to
  withdraw. Icon shape must not be altered, which is why vendor artwork here is
  embedded byte-for-byte with no optimisation pass.

## What is deliberately not here

75 products are catalogued with a URL and a fetch command but no bytes,
because no permissively licensed mark for them exists, or because the brand owner asked
for it to be removed from the source we would otherwise use. Shipping them anyway would
contradict the reason the rest of this file can be written honestly.

See `references/pack-index.md` for the full list.

## Corrections

If you own one of these marks and want it removed or corrected, open an issue at
<https://github.com/mouadja02/arkitect/issues> and it will be taken out of the next
release. Nothing here is load-bearing enough to argue about.
